# Insider Trading Tracker

Who's buying and selling their own company's stock? Search any public company to see insider trading activity from SEC Form 4 filings.

**Live site:** https://GreatTombProductions.github.io/insider-trading/

## What this is

A static search tool showing insider buying and selling activity for the top 500 public companies. Corporate executives, board members, and major shareholders must file Form 4 with the SEC within 2 business days of trading their company's stock. This tool aggregates those filings and shows:

- Open market purchases and sales (the most informative signals)
- Buy/sell ratios
- Notable trades over $1M
- Insider breakdown by role (CEO, CFO, Director, 10%+ Owner)
- Individual transaction histories with expandable detail

## What this isn't

- Not investment advice
- Not a real-time feed (data is periodic)
- Not affiliated with the SEC
- Not a platform with maintenance obligations

## Data sources

- [SEC EDGAR Form 4 Filings](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&type=4) (public domain)
